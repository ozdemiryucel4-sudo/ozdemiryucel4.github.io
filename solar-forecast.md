## What is this topic about?
Hello! My presentation is about solar panels and weather. Solar energy is very nice, but weather changes all the time. When it is cloudy or rainy, solar panels do not make enough electricity. 

To fix this, we can use a computer program with machine learning. The program looks at past weather data (like temperature and sun hours) and tries to guess how much energy the solar panels will make. 

## Main Parts of My Presentation
1. **The Problem:** Weather changes fast and solar power can be unstable.
2. **The Method:** Using simple machine learning models to look at weather data and learn patterns.
3. **The Result:** Helping power systems understand how much energy they will get.
